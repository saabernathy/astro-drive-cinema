# Astro-Drive Cinema

A retro 1950s Googie-style mid-century speculative cinema and atomic sci-fi movie review site.

## Overview
Astro-Drive Cinema explores mid-century speculative fiction films from the Atomic Age, presented with classic drive-in marquee styling, diner card layouts, and retro Googie architectural aesthetics.

## Features
- **Curated Atomic Reviews**: Reviews and critiques of 1950s science fiction classics including *The Day the Earth Stood Still*, *The War of the Worlds*, *Forbidden Planet*, *The Incredible Shrinking Man*, *Invasion of the Body Snatchers*, and *Them!*.
- **Mid-Century Googie Aesthetics**: Boomerang curves, neon marquees, atomic sparkles, and diner-card styling.
- **The Projection Booth**: Dedicated about page detailing the archival mission and review criteria.
